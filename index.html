Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Personal Computer Technician position at St. Luke's Hospital. Technical support has been a continuous part of my career, from internal help desk and remote desktop support to maintaining business networks, workstations and production equipment. I bring hands-on experience with computer hardware, Windows and Mac systems, Active Directory, user accounts, connectivity troubleshooting and software configuration.

In my current work with MA2K, I lead technical support for PCs, Macs, embroidery machines and heat presses. I also established and maintain a network connecting teams in the United States and Guinea for shared files, design collaboration and client order fulfillment. Keeping those systems operational requires diagnosing problems quickly, communicating clearly and understanding how technical interruptions affect the people depending on them.

At Experian Data Quality, I supported employee accounts, hardware, software and internal network access through the help desk while assisting customers with software integrations. At LogMeIn, I used Rescue to troubleshoot customer computers remotely and served as a technical support team lead. My work at Honey and Thrive Market included end-user support, technical issue documentation, connectivity troubleshooting and escalations.

Southcoast's emphasis on reliable technical service and responsive care aligns with how I approach support: identify the issue, restore function, communicate the resolution and follow through. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute that experience to the team supporting clinical and administrative staff.
